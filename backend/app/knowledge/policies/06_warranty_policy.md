# Warranty Policy
Version: 1.4
Effective: 2024-01-01

## Standard Warranty Periods
- Electronics: 1 year from delivery date
- Appliances: 1 year from delivery date
- Furniture: 6 months from delivery date
- Clothing and accessories: 30 days (defects only, not wear and tear)
- Footwear: 90 days from delivery date
- Jewelry: 1 year for manufacturing defects

## What Warranty Covers
- Manufacturing defects
- Component failures under normal use
- Material defects
- Electrical/mechanical malfunctions

## What Warranty Does NOT Cover
- Physical damage from misuse, drops, or accidents
- Water damage (unless product is rated water-resistant)
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) that don't affect functionality
- Battery degradation below 80% capacity after 6 months (normal for electronics)

## Warranty Claim Process
1. Customer provides order ID and description of the defect
2. Photo or video evidence may be requested
3. For items under ₹5,000: Ship replacement, no return needed
4. For items ₹5,000-₹20,000: Replacement after inspection (return required)
5. For items above ₹20,000: Authorized service center repair first, replacement if repair fails

## Extended Warranty
- Available for purchase at time of order (10% of product price)
- Extends warranty by an additional year
- Covers accidental damage (one incident)

## Out of Warranty
- Items beyond the warranty period are not eligible for free repair/replacement
- Customers can be directed to authorized service centers (paid repair)
- Offer a 15% discount on purchasing a replacement product as goodwill
