# Shipping Delay Compensation Policy
Version: 1.3
Effective: 2024-03-01

## Delay Thresholds and Compensation
When an order is delivered later than the expected delivery date:
- 1-2 days late: Apology email + ₹50 store credit
- 3-5 days late: 10% of order value as store credit (max ₹500)
- 6-10 days late: 15% of order value as refund (max ₹1,000)
- More than 10 days late: 20% of order value as refund (max ₹2,000) OR full refund if customer prefers

## Still In Transit (Not Yet Delivered)
If an order is past its expected delivery date but not yet delivered:
- 1-3 days overdue: Inform customer of updated ETA, apologize
- 4-7 days overdue: Offer to file a carrier investigation + ₹100 credit
- 8+ days overdue: Offer full refund OR continue waiting with compensation

## Special Cases
- Natural disasters or government-imposed restrictions: No compensation, but proactive communication required
- Carrier lost package (confirmed): Full refund + 10% goodwill credit
- Wrong address provided by customer: No compensation applicable

## VIP Customer Exceptions
VIP customers receive:
- Double the standard compensation amounts
- Priority carrier investigation (24-hour response)
- Free upgrade to express shipping on next order
- Personal follow-up call from senior representative

## Proactive Notifications
For delays detected before the expected delivery date:
- System should auto-send delay notification to customer
- Include updated ETA and compensation offer
- This reduces complaint volume and improves satisfaction
