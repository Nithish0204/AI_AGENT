# VIP Customer Handling Policy
Version: 1.1
Effective: 2024-01-01

## VIP Identification
A customer is classified as VIP when:
- Lifetime spending exceeds ₹200,000
- OR they have 25+ completed orders
- OR they are manually flagged as VIP by account management
- VIP status is indicated in the customer profile (tier: "vip")

## VIP Service Standards
- Response time: Under 2 minutes (vs. 5 minutes standard)
- Resolution time: Under 4 hours (vs. 24 hours standard)
- Dedicated escalation path to senior representatives
- All VIP cases get human review (never fully automated)
- Proactive outreach for any order issues

## VIP Compensation Guidelines
VIP customers receive enhanced compensation:
- Shipping delay: Double standard compensation
- Product issues: Full refund + replacement (both, not either)
- Service failures: ₹1,000 minimum goodwill credit
- Any escalation: Personal apology call from supervisor within 24 hours

## IMPORTANT: VIP Override Rule (CONFLICTS WITH REFUND POLICY)
For VIP customers, the standard 30-day return window is extended to 60 days.
This overrides the standard refund policy's 30-day limit.
When there is a conflict between the VIP policy and other policies,
the VIP policy takes precedence for VIP-tier customers.

## VIP Communication Style
- Use customer's name in every interaction
- Reference their history ("As a valued customer since 2021...")
- Avoid scripted/generic responses
- Offer direct contact details of their assigned representative
- Follow up after resolution to confirm satisfaction
