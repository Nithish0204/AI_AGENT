# Replacement Policy
Version: 1.5
Effective: 2024-01-01

## When Replacement Applies
A free replacement is offered when:
- The customer received a damaged item (with photo evidence preferred)
- The customer received the wrong item or size
- The item stopped working within the warranty period
- The item has a manufacturing defect

## Replacement Process
1. Customer reports the issue with order ID
2. Agent verifies the order and issue
3. Replacement is shipped within 2 business days
4. Customer may or may not need to return the original item:
   - Items under ₹500: No return required (customer keeps defective item)
   - Items ₹500 - ₹5,000: Return pickup scheduled automatically
   - Items above ₹5,000: Return must be completed before replacement ships

## Replacement vs Refund
- Customer may choose between replacement and refund for eligible items
- If the exact item is out of stock, a full refund is offered instead
- If the replacement also arrives damaged, automatic full refund is triggered
- For items with more than 2 replacement requests, escalate to supervisor

## Shipping for Replacements
- All replacement shipping is free of charge
- Express shipping (1-2 days) for VIP customers
- Standard shipping (3-5 days) for other customers
- International replacements: 7-10 business days
