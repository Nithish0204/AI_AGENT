# Cancellation Policy
Version: 1.2
Effective: 2024-01-15

## Cancellation Windows
- Before order processing (within 1 hour): Free cancellation, instant refund
- After processing, before shipping: Free cancellation, refund within 24 hours
- After shipping, before delivery: Cancellation accepted, refund minus ₹99 shipping fee
- After delivery: Not a cancellation — standard return policy applies

## How to Cancel
Customers can cancel through:
1. The app or website (self-service, instant)
2. Customer support chat or call
3. Email (processed within 4 hours)

## Non-Cancellable Orders
The following orders cannot be cancelled after processing:
- Custom-made or personalized items
- Perishable goods already dispatched
- Flash sale items (marked as final sale)
- International orders after customs clearance

## Partial Cancellation
- Customers may cancel individual items from a multi-item order
- Shipping fee is only waived if the entire order is cancelled
- Partial cancellation refund is processed for the specific items only

## Automatic Cancellation
Orders are automatically cancelled if:
- Payment verification fails within 24 hours
- Item goes out of stock before shipping
- Delivery attempt fails 3 times (returned to warehouse)

In all auto-cancellation cases, full refund is processed automatically.
