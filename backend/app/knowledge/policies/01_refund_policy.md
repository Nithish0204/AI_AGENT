# Refund Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective: 2024-01-01

## Eligibility Criteria
Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- The item is returned within 30 days of delivery
- The item is in its original packaging and unused condition
- The item is not in the non-returnable category (see section below)

For damaged or defective items, the 30-day window is extended to 45 days from delivery.

## Refund Amounts
- Full refund: Item returned within 30 days in original condition
- Partial refund (80%): Item returned within 30 days but opened/used
- Late delivery compensation: 10% of order value, maximum ₹500
- Damaged item: Full refund OR free replacement (customer's choice)
- Wrong item shipped: Full refund AND free correct item shipped

## Processing Time
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after return is received
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 days to appear
- UPI/wallet refunds are processed within 24 hours

## Non-Returnable Items
The following categories cannot be returned or refunded:
- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Personal hygiene products (opened)
- Customized/personalized items
- Digital downloads
- Gift cards

## Refund Amount Limits
- Orders under ₹2,000: Can be auto-approved by the system
- Orders ₹2,000 - ₹10,000: Require supervisor approval
- Orders above ₹10,000: Require manager-level approval
- Maximum single refund: ₹250,000

## Cancellation Refunds
- Before shipping: Full refund, processed within 24 hours
- After shipping, before delivery: Full refund minus shipping charges (₹99)
- After delivery: Standard return policy applies
